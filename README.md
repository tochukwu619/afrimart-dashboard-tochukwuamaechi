# afrimart-dashboard-tochukwuamaechi


Tool used: Power BI
Dataset: AfriMart_Sales_Dataset.xlsx (700 transactions, 10 countries, 7 products)
## Key findings
1. Rice (50kg Bag) is the most profitable product, generating ₦955.84M in profit.
2. Nigeria is the highest revenue-generating country at ₦1.62B.
3. Aug 2026 was the best-performing period with total revenue of ₦538.72M.
